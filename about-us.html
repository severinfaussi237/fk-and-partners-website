FK and Partners

FK and Partners is a company specialized in financing, investment, management and consultancy.

FK is the privileged partner of states, institutions and private individuals seeking capital to carry out their projects.

The firm was created by two sons of Africa with ambition to contribute to the development and the expansion of the continent

FK & Partners

Our mission

Advise, support and energize our public and private partners in a professional and efficient manner to enable them to succeed in their projects.

Make their organization more efficient and productive and their businesses more profitable and stable.

Allow them to integrate the corporate social responsibility policy into their environment and to better understand its multiple impacts on the well-being of human resources aa well as on the economic, social and ethical health of the company

FK & Partners

Our vision

The wind of globalisation has changed, Africa now has a major role to play in the world, and the issues of transformation of societies and organisations, as well as the development of countries, are effective.

To better address these major challenges, FK & Partners has structured itself to provide an appropriate response to the concerns of private and public actors.

We capitalize on the competence and professionalism of our teams, the experience acquired through out our many projects, the expertise, technical capacities and financial resources of our strategic partners, who are among the world leaders.

FK & Partners

Our values

FK & Partners is a civic-minded company with strong and varied values, based on morality, professionalism, excellence, respect for the law and for clients, and finally on human value.

Our principles are based on the values of ethics, loyalty, adaptability, quality of service, innovation, respect and dynamism.

We place people at the heart of our approach. Our operation is distinguished by our collaborative and benevolent approach.
