Food security

Food security

We have developed an ingenious and equitable model for food security programs

Phase 1: We carry out the Exploitation (agricultural, poultry, breeding, fish farming...) on large expanses of suitable land

Phase 2: We build factories and start industrial production

Phase 3: We distribute the products from our factories to the local market

Commodities concerned: Rice, Pasta, Wheat, Sugar, Flour, Corn, Peanuts, Vegetable Oils...etc

We also produce energy (bioethanol) thanks to the development of the farm.
